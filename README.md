# RSALiveBirths-1998to2024
A dashboard observing parents' ages in South Africa with a particular interest in teenage mom and single mom tends.

## INTRODUCTION
This report is inspired by annual concerns raised by the government of South Africa when media broadcasts Christmas and New Year's births.
In this report you will be able to explore live birth data recorded by Statistics South Africa. 
Statistics South Africa is a government entity that collects, stores and distributes statistics. Their mission is “Transform the production, coordination and use of statistics through optimization, partnerships and innovation”

## Overview
The dashboard turns over 32 million observations into an interactive dashboard that that looks at trends and facts regarding parents’ ages from 1998 to 2024.

## Method
PowerBI’s powerful Power Query was used extensively to clean and verify the data based on the dataset’s metadata provided by Statistics SA. 
Additionally, DAX functions and filtering were implemented to further clean the data, where for example, filtering of 998 and 999 entries in observations of fathers’ age.
The dashboard background was designed in PowerPoint.

## Observations
South Africa is still struggling with child and teenage pregnancy, as well as other social issues such as absent fathers. 
At the time of publishing it still remains a mystery why there was a sharp decline in the number of recorded fathers.

# Conclusion
Teenage pregnancy numbers are slowly decreasing, but single mothers are on the other hand slowly increasing the in the Republic of South Africa.
